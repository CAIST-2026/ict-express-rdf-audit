# ICT_EXPRESS_FIT_ANALYSIS

## Fit with Aims & Scope

The letter is an evaluation-methodology and systems-audit contribution about retrieval over heterogeneous, bilingual structured data. This fits ICT Express because it addresses reproducible retrieval architecture, cross-domain metadata heterogeneity, and measurable system cost rather than a purely NLP task. The scope is explicitly methodological: it does not claim a deployed telecom system.

## Article-type fit

- Short structured letter format retained.
- Final PDF: 6 pages.
- Abstract: approximately 230 words (<250).
- Five keywords retained.
- Single-blind conventions retained; no acknowledgements that identify authors.
- CRediT, COI, Funding, Generative-AI declaration, and Code/data availability included.

## Strength after revision

The revision materially improves fit by:

1. adding a compact related-work positioning paragraph;
2. replacing the confounded latency table with a unified workload;
3. adding authentic Wikidata methodology-transfer evidence with explicit scope limits;
4. consolidating statistical tests, effect sizes, and intervals;
5. correcting two bibliography DOI/page errors;
6. removing the unsupported O(1) complexity claim.

## Remaining risk

The main benchmark is synthetic and the real-RDF check is small. This is disclosed in the abstract, threats, and conclusion. The paper should not be described as a real ICT deployment study. The realistic submission category is an evidence-constrained measurement letter, not a new-retrieval-method paper.
